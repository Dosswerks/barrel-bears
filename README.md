# Barrel Bears

## The Story

Deep in the forest, Mama Bear has climbed to the top of the tallest trees with her cubs. One by one, the little cubs tumble from the branches — and it's up to you to catch them!

Move your barrel beneath the falling cubs to rescue them before they hit the ground. The more cubs you save, the faster Mama Bear moves and the quicker the cubs fall. How many can you rescue?

## How to Play

- **Desktop:** Move your mouse left and right to position the barrel. Click to start.
- **Mobile:** Drag your finger to move the barrel. Tap to start.
- **Restart:** Tap the screen, click, or press R after game over.

## Scoring

- Each cub caught = 1 point
- You start with 3 barrels (lives)
- Miss a cub and you lose a barrel
- Rescue enough cubs to advance to the next level — Mama Bear gets faster!
