# 🛢️🐻 Barrel Bears 🐻🛢️  
*A splashy, fast-moving rescue game inspired by classic arcade fun*

---

## 📖 Once Upon a Time in the Mountain Forest…

High in the misty mountains, where pine trees sway and waterfalls sing, there lives a busy Bear family.

Mama Bear is brave, strong, and very determined. Every morning, she and her fluffy cubs climb the tallest trees in the forest, searching for sweet, golden honey hidden among the branches. The cubs giggle and wobble as they climb, their paws sticky and their hearts full.

But today is a special day.

The cubs have decided they’re ready for their **first big swim**.

There’s just one problem—there are *so many* cubs, and they’re all eager to get down **right now**. Mama Bear does what any clever bear would do: she gently tosses her cubs down from the trees toward a big barrel of cool, refreshing water.

That’s where **you** come in.

Mama Bear needs your help to safely catch every cub before they tumble onto the forest floor. Slide the barrel, keep your balance, and make sure every cub gets a splashy landing.

Miss too many, and the forest fun comes to an end.  
Catch them all, and it’s a legendary pool day in the mountains!

---

## 🎮 About the Game

**Barrel Bears** is a fast-paced arcade-style game inspired by Atari’s classic *Kaboom*.

- Mama Bear tosses her cubs
- You move a barrel filled with water
- Your goal is to **catch as many cubs as possible**
- The game speeds up as you go—stay sharp!

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

### ✅ Objective
Catch the falling bear cubs in your barrel of water before they hit the ground.

---

### 💻 Desktop Controls
- **Move the Barrel:**  
  Move your **mouse left and right** to slide the barrel across the screen.
- **Start / Restart the Game:**  
  Press the **`R` key** on your keyboard.

---

### 📱 Mobile Controls
- **Move the Barrel:**  
  **Slide your finger left and right** on the screen.
- **Start the Game:**  
  **Tap the screen** to begin.

---

## 🐾 Tips for Aspiring Bear Rescuers
- Watch Mama Bear closely—she doesn’t toss every cub the same way!
- Stay centered, but be ready to move fast.
- As the game gets quicker, smooth movements matter more than big swipes.

---

## 🌲 Final Word

Mama Bear is counting on you.  
The cubs are fearless (and a little bouncy).  
The water barrel is ready.

Good luck, brave rescuer—  
and welcome to **Barrel Bears**! 🐻💦
